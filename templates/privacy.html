1. Introduction

WaveZA ("we", "us", "our") operates the WaveZA platform. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform.

2. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to enhance your experience:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile details
  • Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, and timestamps
  • Communication: Messages and support requests you submit to us
  • Content Data: Artworks, poems, and creative content you upload

3. Use of Data

WaveZA uses the collected data for purposes including:

  • Providing and maintaining our Service
  • Notifying you about changes to our Service
  • Allowing you to participate in interactive features
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Gathering analytics to improve our Service
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent activities

4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We use industry-standard encryption methods and follow best practices to protect your information from unauthorised access and usage. Your data is stored on secure servers with multiple layers of protection.
