Pain Is Better

(99 Reviews)

You are not a monster,
Until the world asks you to be one

In the chapters of life, pain will always hold a page
Sometimes, the process of progress is an uncomfortable phase
And while you're in the middle of it all, the best you can do is show a smiley face
Pain is invisible
And so it is also inevitable

How often we lie to each other and call each other 'friends'
But I'm sure even the dictionary is pissed at us for misusing that word
When you're in pain, don't pretend to be ok
So, let it stay
Let it stay while you spend a smile with a friend, a real friend
Show them that you're not ok
The ones who know you're alone
From the way you pick up the phone
That lower tone that you own
They hear it

But then again...
What good is talking when all they are going to say is,
"Oh! Don't cry. You are a man now" Oh! So I'm a man now?
But what good is my manliness if my mother is gonna bury her son because the world was asking too much of me?

You're all asking too much of me because I can't meet your expectations when I'm trying to be somebody
Mother misses her little boy who was gonna grow up to be somebody
Now, they all say "I think you should go and see somebody"
Man, I can't go to sleep without asking myself "Why aren't I enough"

It's always "Man up" until there's another man down
RIP hashtags food the streets
My head covered in neat locks,
But my chest is pinned down under many locks
I'm dripping in designer but I'm drowning
In a sea of self-loathing

Depression is already beefing another man now
My bro's got waves in his hair
But there's water in his chest
He's dripping in designer but he's drowning
In a sea of self confusion

They say we should enjoy the ride, enjoy your life, you only live once"
Yes, we live once
So, why not embrace the pain, too?
Even if I gotta take, for the pain, two
Novocaine be the real friend I needed all my life
Now, I can't sleep without anti-depressants

This invisible pain has me seeing doctors
Maybe if it could be pain I can see, I wouldn't be wasting money on chem labs and doctors
But I'd be getting better blades and sharper razors
Maybe even buy them polish sometimes
Because It's always "Man up"
Until there's another man down

Joy has become a stranger so much I fear each time I see her
I cuddled with my PTSD for so long she called me a cheater and said she ain't coming back
Happiness saw it happen, maybe that's why she up and left
I fell in love with my pain and regrets so much I feel lonely without them
I smile every time a person says they're proud of me
But really I just wanna knock your lights out
I'm so glad I learnt that it's a crime
And that's why I have this purple heart

A purple heart I got from beating myself up over expectations I can't meet
And thus, I'm a failure to the manhood
That's why "real men" don't shed a tear
And that's why I've been leaving a trail of blood with each step I take
Maybe the pain really is better
It'll never make you question your existence
